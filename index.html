brew log · first-time setup
This app uses Google Sheets as its database — free, in your own Google account.

1 — Open Google Sheets, create a blank spreadsheet named brewlog.

2 — Click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete existing code, paste toka-brewlog-backend.gs, save.

3 — Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app → Anyone → Deploy. Copy the URL.

4 — Paste it below. Share the same URL with your team.

Brewing process
First fermentation
Where every brew begins — start date, vessel, sugar, tea, water & SCOBY.
Infusion
The spice steep between fermentations — source vessel, spices used & when to strain.
Bottling
Second fermentation, bottle by bottle — source brew, sugar, yeast, spices & fridge date.
Faucet timer
Set a flow rate once, then calculate how long to keep the faucet open for any volume.
Quality Control
Labo
Brix %, pH & temperature readings for every batch, logged over time.
Tasting
Group bottles by recipe — same sugar, yeast, flavour & days — and log how each one tasted.
Lineage
Every batch, start to finish — the full batch → infusion → bottle lineage, days per stage & readings.
Business
Daily operations
What needs doing today — what’s fermenting, when to brew next, when to fridge — plus your own running to-do list.
Production timeline
Everything that isn’t brewing — logistics, labelling, procurement & the like — mapped as best- and worst-case timelines.
Expenses
Every cost logged and categorized — receipts attached, running totals & your cash balance always in view.
